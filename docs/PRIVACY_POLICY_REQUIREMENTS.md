# Privacy Policy Requirements Matrix

The application privacy policy published for store submission must cover:

- [x] **Specific Purpose:** Account verification, referral matching, and peer wallet transfers on Quebix backend.
- [x] **Data Types Collected:** Display Names and Normalized Phone Numbers (`+91...`).
- [x] **Server Transmission:** Transmitted securely to `api.quebix.in` over HTTPS.
- [x] **Opt-Out Rights:** Users may tap "Not now" or decline consent without losing access to non-contact app features.
- [x] **Erasure Rights:** Users can request deletion of synced contact data at any time in Settings.
- [x] **No Third-Party Sale or Advertising Use:** Contact data is never shared with third-party advertisers or data brokers.
